# Privacy Policy for Psalter 2

**Last updated:** July 21, 2026

**Psalter 2** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Automatically Collected
We do not collect any personal identification information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). However, to ensure app performance and improve user experience, we use **Google Firebase** services which automatically collect certain non-personal diagnostic and usage data:

* **Firebase Analytics & Events:** Collects basic usage statistics, such as app opens, feature engagement, device model, operating system version, and general regional location.
* **Firebase Crashlytics:** Collects crash logs, stack traces, and system state information when the app encounters an error or crashes.

### B. File Downloads & Storage
Our app allows you to view and download a predefined set of files to your device. 

* Files downloaded through the app are stored locally on your device.
* We do **not** scan, collect, view, or upload any personal files stored on your device.

## 2. Accounts and Authentication
Our app does **not** require any user registration, account creation, or sign-in. You can use the full functionality of the app anonymously.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
The non-personal data collected via Firebase is used solely to:

* Monitor and maintain app performance and stability.
* Identify and fix bugs or crash issues.
* Understand general usage patterns to improve app features.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We use Google Firebase services to manage analytics and crash reporting. These third-party services process data under their own privacy policies:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Firebase Privacy and Security Terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your data with any other third parties or advertisers.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because we do not collect personal accounts or store user data on external servers, there is no personal profile data to delete. You can remove all locally downloaded files and cached analytics data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
